# Organising Secretary — Workflow & Handover Guide

This document explains how the Organising Secretary and the Secretary work together, and
how the app mirrors that. Use it when training new officials or handing the project over
to the group.

---

## 1. The mental model

Think of it this way:

> **The Organising Secretary is the brains and muscle behind the event.**
> **The Secretary is the official voice of the group.**

Both are secretaries, but they serve different halves of the same job. The app keeps the
two halves separate so members never get confused.

| | Organising Secretary (Simon) | Secretary / Vice Chair |
|---|---|---|
| Job | Plan events, venues, meetings, mentorship | Communicate officially with members |
| Tools in the app | Events & drives, Meetings, Mentorship | Announcements |
| Sees member contacts? | No | Yes (Directory / membership register) |
| Talks to members officially? | No | Yes — the official communicators |

---

## 2. The two-role split

### Step 1 — The Planning Phase (Organising Secretary)

- The Organising Secretary checks out venues and gets price quotes (tents, catering,
  decorations, transport).
- They design the **programme** for the event (e.g. the Fun Day or the end-of-year party).
- Everything is captured under **Events & drives**: venue, date, time, goal, programme,
  budget lines with estimated costs, and co-ordination contact.
- Meetings (including **Google Meet** sessions) are scheduled from the **Meetings &
  agenda** tab.
- Annual mentorship visits to the three neighbourhood schools are planned from the
  **Mentorship** tab.
- When an event or visit is done, it is marked **Completed** so the history builds up.

### Step 2 — The Communication Phase (Secretary & Vice Chair)

- The **Secretary** drafts and publishes the official notices and meetings' minutes using
  the **Announcements** tab.
- The **Vice Chair** also has announcements, so official communication can still go out
  whenever the Secretary is away.
- Published announcements appear on every member's dashboard.

### Why this division of labour matters

1. **Prevents confusion.** If every official sent messages, members wouldn't know who to
   reply to. With the Secretary (and Vice Chair) as the official communicators, members
   always know official information comes from one place.
2. **Protects privacy.** The Secretary maintains the master membership register. The
   Organising Secretary doesn't need to stress about managing contact lists — they just
   focus on making sure the event logistics are perfect.
3. **Focus.** Planning and communicating each have one home. The Organising Secretary
   plans; the group hears about it officially through announcements.

## 3. How the Organising Secretary portal is organised

| Tab | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Overview** | Upcoming events, completed events, upcoming meetings, next meeting, role mandate |
| **Events & drives** | Plan events: venue, budget quotes, programme, status (Planned → Completed / Cancelled) |
| **Meetings & agenda** | Schedule the group's meetings — including Google Meet sessions (default venue) |
| **Mentorship** | The annual mentorship outreach to Nyakahura Primary, Mugechi Kaboro Primary and Nyakahura Secondary — one visit per school per year |

### Event statuses

1. **Planned** — being prepared (venues, quotes, programme).
2. **Completed** — the event happened (the Secretary already notified members).
3. **Cancelled** — not happening.

---

## 4. The annual mentorship programme

The group runs a mentorship outreach to three neighbourhood schools every year:

- **Nyakahura Primary School**
- **Mugechi Kaboro Primary School**
- **Nyakahura Secondary School**

The **Mentorship** tab tracks one visit per school per year:

- Plan a visit for a school and year → it becomes a Mentorship event (annual).
- Past completed visits are listed per school as history so the record builds up over time.
- Members can see upcoming visits and the completed count on their own dashboard
  ("Mentorship & community").

---

## 5. Training tips

- **Demo a full cycle end to end:** plan a Fun Day in Events & drives, schedule a Google
  Meet meeting, plan a mentorship visit, then publish an announcement from the
  Secretary's portal and see it appear on a member dashboard.
- **Emphasise the rule:** the Organising Secretary plans; the Secretary (and Vice Chair)
  are the only official voice to members.
- **Every office is a role, not a person.** If the organising secretary changes, the next
  official signs in with the same role account and picks up exactly where the last one
  left off.
